# Tour Management System - Workflow Documentation

## Step 1: Tour Quotation Creation

### Overview
The tour quotation creation is the initial step in the tour management workflow. It establishes the foundational tour record with client details, dates, and service requirements.

---

### Routes
- **Main Form**: `GET /tour/quotation`
- **Create Action**: `POST /tour/quotation/insert_consignee`
- **Timeline View**: `GET /tour/quotation/timeline/{manual_id}`

---

### Controller & Method
**Controller**: `App\Controllers\Tour\NewTourController`
**Method**: `insert_consignee()`

---

### Tables Affected

#### 1. **consignee** (Primary Tour Record)

#### 2. **invoice_no** (ID Generation)

#### 3. **tour_quotations** (Timeline/Itinerary - Auto-created)
Created automatically by `add_remove_days_in_qt_table()` method. One record per tour day.

### Metrics Tracked

#### Primary Metrics
1. **Manual ID**: `consignee.manual_id` (e.g., "AH 1174")
2. **Tour ID**: `consignee.tour_id` (System-generated numeric)
3. **Created By**: `consignee.create_user` (Username from session)
4. **Created Date**: `consignee.create_time` (Full datetime: Y-m-d H:i:s)

#### Workflow Tracking
```sql
-- Get tour creation audit trail
SELECT
    manual_id,
    tour_id,
    consignee AS tour_name,
    create_user AS created_by,
    create_time AS created_at
FROM consignee
WHERE tour_id = 'TOUR_ID';
```

## Step 2: Quotation to Booking Conversion & Invoice Generation

### Overview
After finalizing the tour timeline, the quotation is converted to a confirmed booking by generating an invoice for the client.

---

### Routes
- **Invoice Form**: `GET /accounts/debtor_invoice_new?tour_id={tour_id}`
- **Generate Invoice**: `POST /accounts/debtor_invoice_new/complete_payment`

---

### Controller & Method
**Controller**: `App\Controllers\Accounts\DebtorInvoiceNewController`
**Method**: `complete_payment()`

---

### Tables Affected

#### 1. **tour_quotations**
- `confirm` = 1 (Booking confirmed)
- `verify` = 1 (Verified)

#### 2. **consignee**
- `verify` = 1 (Tour verified)
- `amend_inv` = Invoice number

#### 3. **debtor_invoice_log** (Invoice Record)
- `invoice_no` - Generated invoice number (e.g., "AH-1234-07-26")
- `tour_id` - Links to tour
- `agent` - Customer/agent code
- `date` - Invoice date
- `room_cost` / `transport_cost` - Cost breakdown
- `doller_rate` / `dollar_amount` - Currency details
- `credit_balance` - Total invoice amount (LKR)
- `consignee` - Tour name

#### 4. **account_runing** (Accounting Entries - 2 records)
- **DR Entry**: Customer account debit
- **CR Entry**: Income account credit
- Common fields: `Invoice_Num`, `index_no` (tour_id), `Cashier`, `Dt`

#### 5. **invoice_no** (ID Generation)
- Auto-increments debtor invoice counter

---

### Metrics Tracked

#### Primary Metrics
1. **Invoice Number**: `debtor_invoice_log.invoice_no` (e.g., "AH-1234-07-26")
2. **Invoice Date**: `debtor_invoice_log.date`
3. **Generated By**: `account_runing.Cashier` (Username from session)
4. **Generated Timestamp**: `account_runing.Dt` (Y-m-d H:i:s)

#### Workflow Tracking
```sql
-- Get invoice generation audit trail
SELECT
    dil.invoice_no,
    dil.tour_id,
    dil.date AS invoice_date,
    dil.credit_balance AS total_amount,
    ar.Cashier AS generated_by,
    ar.Dt AS generated_at
FROM debtor_invoice_log dil
LEFT JOIN account_runing ar ON ar.Invoice_Num = dil.invoice_no
WHERE dil.tour_id = 'TOUR_ID'
LIMIT 1;
```

## Step 3: Hotel Reservation Process (Two-Stage Approval)

### Overview
Hotel reservations require two-stage approval: first acceptance with amount setting, then confirmation with hotel details.

---

### Stage 1: Invoice Acceptance & Amount Setting

#### Routes
- **Accept Invoice**: `POST /tour/tour_accounts`

#### Controller & Method
**Controller**: `App\Controllers\Tour\TourAccountsController`
**Method**: `accept_invoice()`

#### Tables Affected

##### 1. **consignee**
- `inv_alert_status` = 1 (Accepted)
- `accepted_user` - Username who accepted
- `accepted_date` - Acceptance timestamp

##### 2. **inv_alert_log** (Audit Trail)
- `tour_id` / `manual_id` / `inv_no` - Reference IDs
- `action` = "accepted"
- `action_user` - Username
- `action_date` - Timestamp

#### Metrics Tracked
1. **Accepted By**: `consignee.accepted_user`
2. **Accepted Date**: `consignee.accepted_date` (Y-m-d H:i:s)

---

### Stage 2: Hotel Booking Confirmation

#### Routes
- **Send Booking Email**: `POST /dashboard2/hotel_management/hotel_reservation`
- **Confirm Reservation**: `POST /dashboard2/hotel_management/confirm_reservation`

#### Controller & Method
**Controller**: `App\Controllers\Tour\HotelManagementController`
**Methods**:
- `hotel_reservation()` - Sends booking email
- `confirm_reservation()` - Confirms with hotel details

#### Tables Affected



##### 2. **tour_quotations** (Confirmation)
- `booking_confirmed_flag` = 1 (Confirmed)
- `hotel_advance` - Payment amount (LKR)
- `total_hotel_doller` - Dollar amount
- `confirmation_email` - Uploaded file name
- `confirmation_reference` - Reference number
- `extra_hotel_fee` - Extra charges
- `sglfoc` / `dblfoc` / `tplfoc` - FOC room counts

##### 3. **account_runing** (Accounting - 2 records)
- **DR Entry**: Hotel Charges account (4-8-106-10386)
- **CR Entry**: Hotel supplier account
- Common: `index_no` (tour_id), `Cashier`, `Dt`, `advances_pay` = 22

##### 4. **emails** (Email Log)
- Records sent booking emails with thread tracking

#### Metrics Tracked

##### Booking Email Sent
1. **Sent Date**: Email timestamp
2. **Email Thread**: For tracking responses

##### Confirmation
1. **Confirmed By**: `account_runing.Cashier`
2. **Confirmed Date**: `account_runing.Dt` (Y-m-d H:i:s)
3. **Confirmation Reference**: `tour_quotations.confirmation_reference`

#### Workflow Tracking
```sql
-- Get hotel reservation audit trail
SELECT
    c.tour_id,
    c.manual_id,
    c.accepted_user,
    c.accepted_date,
    tq.hotel_booking_flag,
    tq.booking_confirmed_flag,
    tq.hotel_advance,
    tq.total_hotel_doller,
    tq.confirmation_reference,
    ar.Cashier AS confirmed_by,
    ar.Dt AS confirmed_at
FROM consignee c
LEFT JOIN tour_quotations tq ON tq.quotation_id = c.tour_id
LEFT JOIN account_runing ar ON ar.index_no = c.tour_id AND ar.advances_pay = 22
WHERE c.tour_id = 'TOUR_ID'
LIMIT 1;
```

## Step 4: Driver Advance Payment (Three-Stage Process)

### Overview
Driver advance payments go through three stages: request submission, authentication/approval, and actual payment processing.

---

### Stage 1: Advance Request Submission

#### Routes
- **Request Form**: `GET /dashboard2/transport/driver_allow/advance_request?tour_id={tour_id}`
- **Submit Request**: `POST /dashboard2/transport/driver_allow/advance_request_add`

#### Controller & Method
**Controller**: `App\Controllers\NewDashboard\DriverAllocationController`
**Method**: `advanceRequestSubmit()`

#### Tables Affected

##### 1. **driver_advance_request**
- `tour_id` / `manual_id` - Tour reference
- `driver` / `driver_name` - Driver details
- `amount` - Requested amount
- `username` - Who submitted request
- `date` - Request timestamp
- `flag` = 0 (Pending approval)

#### Metrics Tracked
1. **Requested By**: `driver_advance_request.username`
2. **Request Date**: `driver_advance_request.date` (Y-m-d H:i:s)
3. **Request Amount**: `driver_advance_request.amount`

---

### Stage 2: Authentication & Approval

#### Routes
- **Approve Request**: `POST /tour/tour_accounts/driver_advance_payment_request`

#### Controller & Method
**Controller**: `App\Controllers\Tour\TourAccountsController`
**Method**: `driver_advance_payment_request()`

#### Tables Affected

##### 1. **driver_advance_request**
- `flag` = 1 (Approved)
- Creates new record with adjusted amount if changed

##### 2. **payment_request**
- `tour_id` / `manual_id` / `consignee` - Tour details
- `hotel_acc` = Driver code (differentiates from hotel requests)
- `requestAmount` - Approved amount
- `payment_request` = 1 (Request authenticated)
- `payment_request_user` - Approver username
- `payment_request_date` - Approval timestamp
- Bank details: `bank_name`, `branch_name`, `sup_bank_acc`

##### 3. **consignee**
- `payment_request` = 1
- `payment_request_user` - Approver
- `payment_request_date` - Approval timestamp

#### Metrics Tracked
1. **Authenticated By**: `payment_request.payment_request_user`
2. **Authentication Date**: `payment_request.payment_request_date` (Y-m-d H:i:s)
3. **Approved Amount**: `payment_request.requestAmount`

---

### Stage 3: Payment Processing

#### Routes
- **Payment Form**: `GET /accounts/creditor_payment?tour_id={tour_id}`
- **Complete Payment**: `POST /accounts/creditor_payment/complete_payment`

#### Controller & Method
**Controller**: `App\Controllers\Accounts\CreditorPaymentController`
**Method**: `complete_payment()`

#### Tables Affected

##### 1. **account_runing** (Accounting - 2 records)
- **DR Entry**: Driver/supplier account
- **CR Entry**: Payment method account (bank/cash)
- Common fields: `index_no` (tour_id), `Invoice_Num`, `Dt`

##### 2. **invoice_no** (ID Generation)
- Auto-increments acc_id counter

#### Metrics Tracked
1. **Paid Date**: `account_runing.Date`
2. **Payment Timestamp**: `account_runing.Dt` (Y-m-d H:i:s)
3. **Payment Amount**: `account_runing.Dr` (in DR entry)

#### Workflow Tracking
```sql
-- Get driver advance payment audit trail
SELECT
    dar.id AS request_id,
    dar.tour_id,
    dar.driver,
    dar.driver_name,
    dar.amount AS requested_amount,
    dar.username AS requested_by,
    dar.date AS request_date,
    dar.flag AS request_status,
    pr.requestAmount AS approved_amount,
    pr.payment_request_user AS authenticated_by,
    pr.payment_request_date AS authentication_date,
    ar.Dr AS paid_amount,
    ar.Date AS payment_date,
    ar.Dt AS payment_timestamp
FROM driver_advance_request dar
LEFT JOIN payment_request pr ON pr.tour_id = dar.tour_id AND pr.hotel_acc = dar.driver
LEFT JOIN account_runing ar ON ar.index_no = dar.tour_id AND ar.L_Code = dar.driver AND ar.Dr > 0
WHERE dar.tour_id = 'TOUR_ID'
ORDER BY dar.date DESC;
```
